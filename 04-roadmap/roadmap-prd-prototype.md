# Feature Roadmap, Module 4 · StreamLine Spotlight

**Team:** 2 engineers + 1 designer

## Strategic anchors
- **Persona:** The Disillusioned Curated Seeker: a passionate viewer who wants trusted recommendations and hidden gems but no longer trusts StreamLine's discovery experience.
- **Primary metric:** Increase Month 1 retention among Wanderers exposed to Spotlight-style curation from 64% to 79% (+15 percentage points). Why: Directly tied to the biggest business leak. Supported by existing Spotlight retention signals. Closely connected to churn reduction and LTV growth.
- **Moment of misery:** Recommendations feel repetitive and generic, causing browsing fatigue and driving users to external sources for discovery.
- **Guardrail:** 30+ minute session rate must not decline below the current baseline of 11%. Why: Prevents artificially boosting retention without creating real viewer engagement. Ensures users are actually finding and watching content. Homepage → Playback conversion must remain at or above 29%.

## Roadmap
### NOW, 3-week sprint
- **A1 Spotlight Curated Rail**, Directly replaces repetitive recommendations with trusted picks and tests the proven Spotlight retention signal.
- **A3 Hidden Gem Badge**, Makes distinctive content visible and reinforces the persona’s desire to find quality beyond trending titles.

### NEXT, following 1-2 sprints
- **A4 Mood-Based Entry Point**, Directly addresses contextual discovery, but mood taxonomy, content tagging, UX, and filtering exceed a safe sprint scope.
- **A5 Personalized Spotlight Queue**, Highly relevant to the persona, but recreates recommendation complexity before editorial trust is established.
- **A6 Spotlight Digest Email**, Re-engages Wanderers with trusted picks, but depends on personalization, messaging infrastructure, and attribution.

### LATER, backlog
- **A7 Curator Profiles**, Relevant if A1 proves itself succesful. Builds human authority, but following infrastructure is unnecessary until users value the core curated experience.

### ✂ Cut List
- **A2 'Why You'll Love This' Label**, Could reduce decision paralysis, but AI-generated reasoning risks low trust and adds complexity before curation is proven.
- **A8 Watch Party (Spotlight)**, Adds substantial technical scope without addressing repetitive recommendations or individual discovery confidence.
- **A9 Advanced Filter Engine**, Adds browsing controls but increases effort and choice rather than providing trusted, decisive guidance.
- **A10 Offline Download (Spotlight)**, Improves consumption convenience, not discovery, retention leakage, or the persona’s core moment of misery.
