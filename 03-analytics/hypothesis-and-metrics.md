## Evidence
- **Qualitative (M2):** "The 'Because you watched' row is a joke."
 "I trust a recommendation from a friend more than the algorithm."
 "I open the app, scroll for like twenty minutes, and close it without watching anything."
- **Quantitative (M3):** Only 29% of homepage visitors start playback.
Only 11% of visitors reach a 30+ minute session (down from 19%).
Spotlight cohorts retain 12-15 percentage points more users at Month 1 than Full-Library cohorts.
Wanderers show the largest improvement from Spotlight exposure, with 22 percentage points lower churn than non-exposed Wanderers.

## Persona & problem
- **Role:** The Disillusioned Curated Seeker: a passionate viewer who wants trusted recommendations and hidden gems but no longer trusts StreamLine's discovery experience.
- **Goal:** Discover relevant, high-quality content that matches their tastes, mood, and viewing intent.
- **Friction:** Recommendations feel repetitive and generic, causing browsing fatigue and driving users to external sources for discovery.
- **Problem you are solving:** Users cannot confidently discover worthwhile content inside StreamLine and therefore leave the platform to find recommendations elsewhere.

## Outcome & metrics
- **Strategic outcome:** Increase retention by making StreamLine the trusted destination for content discovery rather than just a playback destination.
- **Primary success metric:** Increase Month 1 retention among Wanderers exposed to Spotlight-style curation from 64% to 79% (+15 percentage points).  Why:  Directly tied to the biggest business leak. Supported by existing Spotlight retention signals. Closely connected to churn reduction and LTV growth.
- **Guardrail metric:** 30+ minute session rate must not decline below the current baseline of 11%.  Why:  Prevents artificially boosting retention without creating real viewer engagement. Ensures users are actually finding and watching content. Homepage → Playback conversion must remain at or above 29%.
- **Decision window:** 30 days (one complete Month 0 → Month 1 retention cycle).
