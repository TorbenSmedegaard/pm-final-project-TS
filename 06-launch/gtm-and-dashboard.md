# GTM Strategy & Success Dashboard (Module 6)

## Get your prior work ready
- **The feature you're launching from your M4 PRD, one feature, not a list:** Spotlight Curated Rail (A1): a prominent, curated recommendation rail that surfaces a small set of trusted movie picks, helping users quickly find something worth watching without endless browsing.
- **What your M5 experiment told you shipped / iterating, the evidence behind the launch:** Users consistently engaged more positively with curated recommendations than with large undifferentiated catalogs. The experiment validated that Spotlight reduces decision fatigue, increases confidence in content selection, and improves the likelihood of starting playback. Results support shipping the feature and monitoring real-world engagement.
- **Your persona pull your M2 persona, this anchors your audience:** Disillusioned Curated Seeker: a streaming subscriber overwhelmed by choice who wants trusted recommendations and quick confidence that a selected title will be worth their time.

## Set your goal, then your audience
- **Primary GTM goal awareness · engagement · conversion, pick one:** Engagement
- **Why this goal? what makes this the right goal for this feature right now:** Spotlight is not a new product and does not require market awareness. Its purpose is to change user behavior inside StreamLine by helping users discover content faster and engage more frequently with the platform. Success depends on adoption and repeated use, not acquisition.
- **Target audience the specific segment your goal implies, be precise:** Existing StreamLine subscribers who regularly browse but struggle to choose content, particularly users exhibiting high browsing time and low content-start rates.

## Size your launch
- **Launch tier S (minimal) · M (targeted) · L (multi-channel) · XL (full GTM):** M (Targeted)
- **Justification reach + revenue impact + what silence would risk:** The feature impacts a meaningful segment of the existing user base and directly supports retention goals, but does not justify a large-scale external campaign. Remaining silent risks low feature discovery and slower adoption, while a focused launch can accelerate behavioral change among existing users.
- **Is this a launch or a release? does it need go-to-market, or can it just ship?:** Launch. Although technically a feature release, Spotlight introduces a new discovery experience and user behavior. Without targeted communication and onboarding, many users may never understand its value.

## Choose your top three channels and plan assets
- **Channel 1 owned / earned / paid, and why it reaches your audience:** Owned: In-app homepage placement and onboarding callouts.

Users encounter Spotlight at the exact moment they are looking for content. Contextual in-product messaging provides the most direct path to adoption.
- **Channel 2 owned / earned / paid, and why:** Owned: Email campaign to active subscribers.

Showcases Spotlight as a new way to discover movies and encourages users to revisit the platform and try the curated experience.
- **Channel 3 owned / earned / paid, and why:** Owned: Push notifications and re-engagement messaging.

Targets users who have recently reduced viewing activity and reminds them that StreamLine now helps them find great content faster.
- **Enablement & assets what Sales / CS / Support need, plus the assets to build (one-pager, demo, etc.):** - Product one-pager explaining Spotlight, target persona, and expected user benefits.
- Short demo video showing "browse fatigue" versus the Spotlight experience.
- Updated homepage guidance and onboarding copy.
- FAQ and support brief for Customer Support.
- Analytics dashboard tracking Spotlight impressions, clicks, play starts, and retention impact.
- Internal launch briefing for Marketing, Support, and Product teams.

## Define how you'll know it worked
- **Success metrics 2 to 3 metrics that match your GTM goal:** 1. Spotlight CTR (Click-through Rate)
 Measures whether users find the curated recommendations compelling enough to explore.

2. Spotlight-to-Play Conversion Rate
 Measures whether Spotlight successfully drives users from discovery into actual viewing.

3. 30-Day User Retention for Spotlight Users vs Non-Users
 Measures whether the feature contributes to the broader retention objective.
- **Bad signal to watch for e.g. high reach, zero signups = message-market mismatch:** High Spotlight impressions but low click-through rate.

Users see the rail but do not perceive the recommendations as relevant or worthwhile. This would indicate a recommendation quality and positioning problem rather than a discovery problem.
- **Most likely post-launch decision double-down · iterate · pivot · deprioritize, and what would trigger it:** Iterate.

Most likely outcome is that the feature demonstrates positive adoption but requires tuning of recommendation selection, copy, curation logic, or rail placement.

Double-down trigger: Significant improvement in play-start conversion and retention.

Iterate trigger: Strong visibility but moderate engagement.

Deprioritize trigger: Low engagement despite multiple recommendation and presentation improvements.
