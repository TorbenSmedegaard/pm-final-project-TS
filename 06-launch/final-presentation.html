01 / COVER
Slide 5 · Strategy

Fix discovery before valuable viewers leave.

MODULES 1 + 3
Strategic crisis

StreamLine risks becoming the biggest content library that nobody can easily navigate, causing high-value subscribers to migrate to competitors that offer a better discovery experience.

Moment of misery

Instead of discovering great films inside StreamLine, users spend more time searching for recommendations outside the platform than actually watching content.

Value proposition

StreamLine Spotlight introduces an editorially curated content rail that highlights high-quality and overlooked titles selected by trusted curators. Instead of overwhelming viewers with algorithmic recommendations, Spotlight provides a focused and credible discovery experience that helps users quickly find something worth watching. This increases confidence in content discovery, reduces browsing frustration, and improves viewer retention.

Problem Hook & Value Proposition, Module 1 Scenario: StreamLine Spotlight (B2C) Strategic crisis StreamLine risks becoming the biggest content library that nobody can easily navigate, causing high-value subscribers to migrate to competitors that offer a better discovery experience. Moment of misery Instead of discovering great films inside StreamLine, users spend more time searching for recommendations outside the platform than actually watching content. Problem hook We must reverse rising churn among our most valuable viewers by fixing the discovery experience that drives them outside StreamLine. Value proposition For passionate film lovers, we will provide trusted, expert-led content discovery because StreamLine must strengthen engagement before competitors capture this audience permanently.. Cold-read self-review Yes. The case explicitly states: Engagement has plateaued. Churn is rising. Niche competitors are gaining ground. StreamLine's historical competitive advantage is becoming less effective. This impacts revenue growth, retention, and market leadership. And yes, the misery is systemic. Discovery becomes harder as the catalogue grows, which is not an isolated issue. It is a structural consequence of the product strategy of maximizing content volume. Therefore the problem is likely to affect a meaningful segment of engaged users, especially those who value content quality and discovery. Evidence Qualitative (M2): "The 'Because you watched' row is a joke." "I trust a recommendation from a friend more than the algorithm." "I open the app, scroll for like twenty minutes, and close it without watching anything." Quantitative (M3): Only 29% of homepage visitors start playback. Only 11% of visitors reach a 30+ minute session (down from 19%). Spotlight cohorts retain 12-15 percentage points more users at Month 1 than Full-Library cohorts. Wanderers show the largest improvement from Spotlight exposure, with 22 percentage points lower churn than non-exposed Wanderers. Persona & problem Role: The Disillusioned Curated Seeker: a passionate viewer who wants trusted recommendations and hidden gems but no longer trusts StreamLine's discovery experience. Goal: Discover relevant, high-quality content that matches their tastes, mood, and viewing intent. Friction: Recommendations feel repetitive and generic, causing browsing fatigue and driving users to external sources for discovery. Problem you are solving: Users cannot confidently discover worthwhile content inside StreamLine and therefore leave the platform to find recommendations elsewhere. Outcome & metrics Strategic outcome: Increase retention by making StreamLine the trusted destination for content discovery rather than just a playback destination. Primary success metric: Increase Month 1 retention among Wanderers exposed to Spotlight-style curation from 64% to 79% (+15 percentage points). Why: Directly tied to the biggest business leak. Supported by existing Spotlight retention signals. Closely connected to churn reduction and LTV growth. Guardrail metric: 30+ minute session rate must not decline below the current baseline of 11%. Why: Prevents artificially boosting retention without creating real viewer engagement. Ensures users are actually finding and watching content. Homepage → Playback conversion must remain at or above 29%. Decision window: 30 days (one complete Month 0 → Month 1 retention cycle).

GTM Strategy & Success Dashboard (Module 6) Primary GTM goal: Engagement Why this goal? Spotlight is not a new product and does not require market awareness. Its purpose is to change user behavior inside StreamLine by helping users discover content faster and engage more frequently with the platform. Success depends on adoption and repeated use, not acquisition. Target audience: Existing StreamLine subscribers who regularly browse but struggle to choose content, particularly users exhibiting high browsing time and low content-start rates. Launch tier: M (Targeted) Justification: The feature impacts a meaningful segment of the existing user base and directly supports retention goals, but does not justify a large-scale external campaign. Remaining silent risks low feature discovery and slower adoption, while a focused launch can accelerate behavioral change among existing users. Launch. 06 / LAUNCH
Slide 10 · Story

From feature ideas to discovery trust.

REFLECTION
Friction points

The hardest part was avoiding solution bias and staying focused on the user's actual problem rather than jumping directly to features.

I was initially surprised by how many ideas seemed valuable but did not address the core discovery challenge.

Aha moment

The breakthrough came when I centered the solution around building trust in discovery rather than creating more recommendation functionality.

Key takeaway

The biggest learning was that successful product decisions start with a deep understanding of user needs and measurable outcomes, not feature brainstorming.

What I would do next

If this were a real product initiative, the next step would be to launch an MVP of the Spotlight Curated Rail, validate its impact on retention and engagement, and use the results to guide future investments in personalization and content discovery.
